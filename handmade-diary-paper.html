How to Use This Component

Basic HTML Structure:

<div class="diary-paper-container">
  <div class="diary-paper">
    <!-- Corner botanical accents -->
    <div class="corner-botanical top-left"></div>
    <div class="corner-botanical top-right"></div>
    <div class="corner-botanical bottom-left"></div>
    <div class="corner-botanical bottom-right"></div>
    
    <!-- Your content goes here -->
    <div class="diary-content">
      <!-- Add your text, images, forms, etc. -->
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

Key Features:

  • Responsive: Automatically adjusts to container width with mobile-optimized breakpoints
  • Natural Edges: CSS clip-path creates authentic torn paper appearance
  • Center Spine: Vertical fold shadow with dashed stitching line
  • Botanical Accents: Faded corner elements for authentic handmade feel
  • Content Ready: Clear z-index layers for easy content overlay
  • Aged Appearance: Vintage gradients and ivory base color (#f4efe4)

Customization:

  • Adjust max-width in .diary-paper-container to control size
  • Modify colors in the background gradients for different paper tones
  • Change aspect-ratio for different paper proportions
  • Customize botanical corner positions and styles
  • Adjust padding in .diary-content for content spacing
